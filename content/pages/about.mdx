<Section intro large title="Designer by craft, systems thinker by training.">

I'm Vishnu, a Senior Product Designer at ICD. For the last five years I've designed enterprise SaaS and AI products: complex workflows, agentic systems, search, automation and decision-making tools.

I studied architecture and planning at NIT Bhopal, where I learned to understand a system before trying to change it. I still work that way. I don't see a product as a collection of screens. I start with the people, the business goals and the technical limits, and design the structure that connects them.

AI is a big part of my work now, and my focus is simple: make it useful, understandable and trustworthy. These days I also build what I design, including this site.

Outside work I sketch and paint, mostly live sketching and watercolour. I also play football, travel and go on long bike rides.

<Section large label="How I design" title="Design like a building.">

A building needs solid ground before it goes up. A product needs a real understanding of the work behind it. This is how I get there.

<Points>
  <Point title="Start with the system.">Before anything goes on a screen, I map the people, the dependencies, the goals and the places where things break.</Point>
  <Point title="Bring everyone in early.">I work with product managers, engineers, compliance and business teams from the first brief, so decisions hold up in review and in code.</Point>
  <Point title="Clarity before screens.">I define the product structure and core workflows first, then design the details on top of them.</Point>
  <Point title="Build systems, not one-off screens.">Clear flows, reusable patterns and documented components keep a product consistent from idea to launch, and as it grows.</Point>
  <Point title="Keep people in control.">When AI is involved, I make its work visible and easy to check, so people can trust what it does.</Point>
</Points>

</Section>
